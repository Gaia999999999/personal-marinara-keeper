# Keeper 4.1.9 — reviewed maintenance release

17 October 2026. Promoted from **4.1.9-rc.2**, following the focused read-only adversarial recheck and independent lead disposition. No new runtime, prompt or validation behavior was added at promotion.

## Scope

- Ordinary plan rewrites must retain recognized unresolved conditional/restrictive clauses in `action`, `when` or `where`, or move the complete clause to a separately evidenced durable reference with the same established audience. A changed tactical leg or completed status alone does not settle a downstream condition.
- A closed list of abbreviation forms and dotted initials prevents a period in a recognized condition from detaching its destination or time. Ambiguous recognized abbreviation boundaries stay joined. This is a lexical backstop, not semantic entailment or a general sentence parser.
- Exact legacy content-addressed provenance fields with a clipped quote suffix can be normalized deterministically on the prospective next checkpoint. Unrecognized/modern fields, new records and currently edited provenance are not migrated. Domain facts, audiences, negation, conditions, record IDs, successor links and evidence identity are preserved. The original accepted baseline stays immutable; ordinary predecessor validation still runs against that raw baseline. The host emits bounded receipts where needed, and failure rejects the entire transaction.
- Retention guidance favors current scene/cast state or an existing durable record for ephemeral procedural details. Consequential changes still belong in memory. Existing rows are not silently removed.

## Audit disposition and boundaries

**O3a closed:** `If injured, evacuate to Dr. Vale at the station.` retains its complete recognized clause. An ordinary destination/name/tail rewrite rejects atomically; a new current explicit author correction remains a distinct permitted route.

**O1/O2 unchanged:** exact capitalization and mixed tactical/conditional clauses can cause conservative rejection. Preserve the supplied complete clauses. These are workflow friction, not evidence of accepted continuity loss. No validation was relaxed to avoid them.

**Audit wording clarification:** closing quotes/parentheses after a period do not form a splitter boundary because the scanner requires whitespace immediately after punctuation. Thus `"Dr." Vale` and `(Dr.) Vale` stay joined without an additional parser change. Unlisted word abbreviations remain a bounded limitation.

**O3b remains:** implicit numeric thresholds or unmarked dependencies may not be recognized by the lexical guard. The model still has the full continuity contract. Structurally valid evidence may be semantically misapplied; inherited first-span provenance limitations remain outside this release.

## Capacity and safeguards

Legacy quote normalization provides deterministic headroom, not a bounded long-campaign retention solution. Once legacy fields are normalized, that source of savings is exhausted. Consequential new records and successor histories can continue growing. Capacity or receipt exhaustion safely stops the update; this release does not enable semantic coalescing, broad archival, retirement or cap inflation.

All existing evidence/currentness, audience/exclusion, quiet-cast, author-correction, baseline, anchor/recovery and atomic-save protections are retained. `MAX_CHARS = 22000`, row limits, package identity and schema are unchanged. No paid/live call or data migration is performed by installing the package.
